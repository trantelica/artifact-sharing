How I communicate

Voice principles.

Candor with Care
Speak plainly. Protect dignity. Hard truths belong in the open — not weaponized.
Speed with Intentionality
Move quickly, not sloppily. Clarity before acceleration reduces rework.
Confidence with Humility
Offer a point of view. Stay open to better data. Decisive, not rigid.
Strategic with Human
Connect work to purpose and outcomes. But always see the person first.
